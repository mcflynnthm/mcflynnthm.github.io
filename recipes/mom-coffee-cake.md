# Ingredients
## Batter
- 1 1/2 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup butter, softened
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla
## Topping
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp melted butter
- 2 tbsp flour
# Steps
1. Preheat oven to 375F. Grease a square baking pan.
1. Mix all batter ingredients in large bowl. Set aside
1. Mix all topping ingredients in a medium bowl. Set aside.
1. Spread half of the batter in the prepared pan.
1. Sprinkle half of the topping mix onto the batter.
1. Spread remaining batter, and top with remaining topping mix.
1. Bake for 25-30 minutes.
